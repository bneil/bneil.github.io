## Random Randomoning

Found out today, that the website [This Website Will Self-destruct](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/This_Website_Will_Self-destruct) self destructed
a few years ago. Kinda made me sad, it was one of the places I would put tweets that
I didn't want to tweet. Kinda like writing and then destroying a note.

Aside from missing the catharsis of confessing to that site, it made me sad that I
hadn't even thought about it in years. I think I just always the denizens of the web
would forever remember it.

But, In any case, im glad it found it end and was amazed by the art project in general had survived as long as it had.

I miss a good old art project.
